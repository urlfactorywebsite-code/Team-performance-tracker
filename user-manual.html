All users

1. Quick start

The tracker records daily revenue and leads, compares results with targets, and keeps a history of additions and corrections.

  1. Sign in with the username and password provided by your owner.
  2. Choose the Month and Entry date at the top. Check the date before recording figures.
  3. Review the dashboard cards. Select a card for detailed performance, or a calendar day for that day's results.
  4. In Enter today's figures, enter the additional revenue or lead count in an available row and select Save entry. Owners first switch Edit Off to Edit On.
  5. Wait for Saved OK and check the updated Current value and Daily log.
  6. Select Logout when finished.
Use the header's question-mark User manual button from any app page. It opens this guide in a popup inside the tracker. Select Close or press Escape to return to your current page with your unsaved fields preserved.

The blinking arrow and flame-style New badge highlight the manual button. A small update notice appears on your first visit, then on the first visit of a new day or once 12 hours have passed since it last appeared. Select Open manual to read the guide or Got it to dismiss the notice. This timing is remembered in the current browser.

2. Login and logout

Features: username/password sign-in, a sign-in error message, and access to this manual before signing in.

  1. Enter your Username and Password on the sign-in page.
  2. Select Login, or press Enter in the password field.
  3. After login, confirm your name and role in the header.

If login fails, check the spelling and password capitalization. An inactive account cannot sign in. Ask an owner to check the account or set a new password in Users; there is no self-service password reset screen.

Use Logout in the header to end the session and return to sign-in. Save any figures you intend to keep before logging out.

Managers and employees use the same entry permissions. Team assignment and the owner's user visibility settings determine which entry rows are available.

Month selects the data to display. Entry date selects the day for entries and selected-day figures; choosing a date in another month changes the displayed month. Dashboard filters do not grant editing permission.

4. Dashboard and filters

Features: team and enabled user cards, monthly targets, selected-day results, progress rings, daily/monthly progress bars, variance, and recent-performance bars.

  1. Select the Month and Entry date you want to review.
  2. Use Type to show Teams or Users, Metric for Revenue or Leads, and Currency for USD or INR revenue.
  3. Use Progress to narrow status groups, or Search to find a team or user by name.
  4. Use From date and To date to examine a date range. The range applies to card calculations within the selected month; select each month separately for a multi-month review.
  5. Use quick chips such as Teams only, Users only, Revenue only, or Leads only. Select Clear filters to reset the card filters and date range.

Without a custom range, Month-to-date actual includes the first of the month through the selected Entry date. With a custom range, cards show Range actual and Expected for range. Card filters apply to cards; the calendar, entry table, and Daily log still show their own month/day data.

Reading a card

  • Monthly target: the configured target. Daily target is monthly target divided by the number of calendar days in that month.
  • Selected day: the actual result for the chosen Entry date.
  • Expected to date / Expected for range: daily target multiplied by the number of days in the period.
  • Variance: actual minus expected. Positive means ahead of the expected amount; negative means behind.
  • Ring: daily or monthly progress according to the card's setting. With a custom range it reflects actual against the expected range target. A ring may differ from the status badge.

Status badges use monthly completion, or expected-range completion when a range is set: Top performer at 120% or above; Completed at 100–119%; On target at 85–99%; Slightly below at 60–84%. Below 60%, a positive selected-day result shows Warning; otherwise the badge shows No progress. Progress filters group Completed with On target, and Warning with Slightly below. A zero target produces 0% progress.

How totals roll up: team cards include direct team entries plus entries for assigned users with a matching metric and, for revenue, currency. Assigned manager/owner cards can summarize assigned teams. Those cards overlap with other cards, so do not add every card together as a grand total. Record each result once using your team's agreed team-row or user-row workflow.

5. Performance card details

Select any dashboard card to open its detailed view. The heading identifies the team or user, metric, and currency.

  • Review monthly/daily targets, selected-day actual, period actual, expected result, variance, remaining target, and completion.
  • Compare average per day so far with the required remaining average to understand the pace needed.
  • Review best and worst recorded positive days in the period, Last 7 days through the selected date, and the improving/stable/dropping Trend.
  • Use the Daily chart, Cumulative monthly chart, and Target vs actual baseline to inspect performance. These charts show the month's series, even when card summary figures use a custom range.
  • Read Add/Revoke history for up to 20 relevant adjustments, including date, action, amount, actor, and subject.

Select Close or click the shaded area outside the popup to return. Make changes using the daily entry table or owner edit sidebar.

6. Calendar and day details

The Daily revenue calendar shows the selected month. Each day separates USD, INR, and Leads, shows recorded team/user counts, and displays any Leave marker. The summary above it lists month totals in those same separate units.

  1. Select a day. This also changes Entry date and selected-day dashboard figures.
  2. In the popup, read Team performance and User performance.
  3. Review Entry log for team adjustments and User log for individual adjustments, including who added or revoked an amount.
  4. Select Close or click outside the popup when finished.

When focus is inside the calendar, use ←/→ to move one day and ↑/↓ to move seven days. Navigation can cross month boundaries. Press Enter on a focused day button to open its details.

The date picker also lets you choose a month, year, and day. Press Escape or click outside it to close the picker.

Use the numeric USD, INR, and Leads figures for comparisons. Calendar totals combine direct team entries and user entries separately from card rollups; calendar colors are a quick visual cue, not a currency conversion. Dashboard card filters do not filter the calendar.

7. Daily entries and corrections

Features: available team/user rows, the unit, Current amount, an Amount / Count input, and a separate Revoke input/action. Despite the heading "Enter today's figures", entries use the selected Entry date.

Add a result

  1. Choose the correct Entry date. Owners enable Edit On.
  2. Find the correct Team or User row and check its Unit: USD, INR, or Leads.
  3. Enter a positive additional amount in Amount / Count. You can fill more than one available row.
  4. Select Save entry. Wait for Saved OK, then confirm Current and the Daily log.
Example: Current is USD 100 and you have earned another USD 25. Enter 25 and save; the result becomes USD 125. Entering 125 would add 125 again.

Save entry adds to the saved value; it does not replace it. Blank/zero amounts are skipped. Do not save the same result in both a team row and an assigned user's row, because matching user entries already contribute to the team card.

Correct an amount with Revoke

Owners can revoke entries made by anyone with Edit On or Off. Their entry table includes hidden, inactive, and deleted user rows and inactive teams for corrections. These rows do not enable new additions or restore accounts.

  1. Select the date of the original entry and locate the same Team or User row.
  2. Type the positive amount to remove in that row's Revoke input.
  3. Select that row's Revoke button. It applies the correction immediately; Save entry is for additions.
  4. Check the updated Current value and the recorded revoke in the log. Add the correct amount separately if needed.

You cannot revoke more than the saved direct entry total for that row/date. Managers and employees can revoke only their own remaining net contributions for that day; an owner can correct other users' contributions. For owners, Current in the entry table shows the direct saved amount available to revoke. Team cards may also include assigned user results; correct those contributions in the original user row.

Correction example: you entered 50 leads instead of 40. Revoke 10 on the original row/date. The addition and correction remain visible in history.

Clear fields resets unsaved addition/revoke inputs to zero; it does not delete saved records. Changing the date or rebuilding the view can also reset unsaved inputs, so save before navigating.

If a multi-row save reports an error, check the log and current values before retrying: some rows may already have saved. Retry only missing additions to avoid duplicate amounts.

8. Daily log

The Daily log below the entry table groups saved totals and adjustments by date, with the latest dates first.

  1. Choose the Month you want to inspect.
  2. Select a date heading to expand its details. This also selects that Entry date and updates the dashboard.
  3. Read the user/actor name, Team or User subject, assigned team where shown, metric, action, amount, and any timestamp or note.
  4. Select the heading again to collapse it, or another date to inspect that day.

Added and Revoked are individual adjustments. Saved total is a snapshot, not another addition. Do not add snapshot and adjustment values together.

Owners can select Revoke beside an Added entry in card Add/Revoke history, expanded Daily log, or the calendar Entry/User log, with Edit On or Off. Confirm to remove that entry's full amount from its original team or user and date. The original addition stays visible, a linked correction is recorded, and its button changes to Revoked. Repeated requests do not subtract it again. Saved totals and revoke records have no Revoke button. If the saved daily balance is too low to reverse the full entry, use the entry table for a smaller correction.

9. Edit controls and leave

Owners start in viewing mode. Select Edit Off in the header to switch to Edit On. On Home, the Edit controls sidebar appears alongside the dashboard.

  • + Team / + User: open the corresponding management page.
  • Selected day: changes the same Entry date used by the main page.
  • Day summary: shows separate unit totals and logged team/user counts.
  • Add / Revoke: enter an amount next to a team or enabled user and select its button. Each button saves that individual adjustment immediately.

Mark or clear leave

  1. Choose the Selected day in the sidebar.
  2. Enter an optional Leave note, then select Mark leave.
  3. The date displays a Leave marker in the calendar and its details. To change the note, enter it and select Update leave.
  4. Select Clear leave to remove the marker.

A leave marker applies to the date across the tracker, rather than to a selected employee. It does not remove saved entries or reduce the number of calendar days used for target calculations.

Switch Edit On back to Edit Off when you finish making changes.

Owner · Edit On

10. Team management

Open: enable Edit On, then select Teams in the header or + Team in the sidebar.

Create a team

  1. Enter a Team name and a nonnegative Monthly target.
  2. Choose Revenue or Leads. For Revenue, choose USD or INR; currency is disabled for Leads.
  3. Set Order to control display order; lower numbers appear first.
  4. Choose Daily or Monthly progress mode to control the dashboard ring. The target field remains a monthly target in either mode.
  5. Leave Active checked and select Add team.

Edit or deactivate a team

Update the existing team's row and select that row's Save. Uncheck Active and save to remove it from active team views; check Active and save to reactivate it. Deactivation is not deletion of its saved records.

Targets, metrics, and currency settings affect how dashboard figures are interpreted. Confirm the intended setting before changing a team with historical entries. To give people entry access to the team, assign it to them in Users.

Validation errors appear on the page. Correct the indicated name, target, or metric setting and save again. Use Back Home when finished.

Owner · Edit On

11. User management

Open: enable Edit On, then select Users in the header or + User in the sidebar.

Create an account

  1. Enter a unique Username and a Display name.
  2. Choose the role: Owner, Manager, or Employee.
  3. Set the nonnegative Entry target for personal performance. It is used as a monthly target for the user's card; assigned owner/manager cards can instead use assigned team targets.
  4. Choose Revenue or Leads, and USD/INR for Revenue. Currency is disabled for Leads.
  5. Open the team selector and check the teams this account should be assigned to. Multiple assignments are supported.
  6. Set a password of at least 6 characters.
  7. Choose the visibility options below, then select Add user. New accounts are active.
SettingWhat it does
Show on DashboardDisplays a performance card for this user. Unchecked by default for new users.
Entry listIncludes the user in daily user-entry controls, subject to editing permissions. Checked by default.
Account activeTurn off in Details to disable login and exclude the account from active user-entry data. This switch saves immediately.
Team assignmentsAllow team-row entry access and determine relevant performance rollups. These are separate from the visibility checkboxes.

Edit an account or reset a password

Select Details on a user's row to open their information popup. Review their ID, creation/update dates, username, display name, role, target, currency, sales type, and assigned teams. Edit profile fields or assignments and select Save details.

The animated Show on Dashboard, Entry list, and Account active switches save immediately. They do not save other unsaved profile edits. An inactive user's entire row has a red background and an Inactive label.

Select Reset password from the row or details popup. Enter and confirm a new password of at least 6 characters, then select Reset password. Use Show password to check your input. Passwords currently stored in the account are never displayed. Give the new login details to the intended user through your normal private channel.

Deactivate, reactivate, or delete

Select Deactivate on the row, or turn Account active off in Details, to block login. Use Activate or turn the switch on to restore access. Saved history is retained.

Select Delete on the row, or Delete user in Details, then type the exact username and confirm. The account disappears from user management and login is blocked. Its identity, username, assignments, and entry history are retained for historical records; the username cannot be reused and there is no restore action here. Use Deactivate when you want to reverse the change later.

You cannot deactivate or delete your own current owner account, or remove your own owner role. Only owners can perform these actions. Errors appear in the popup or above the list; failed toggle saves restore the previous switch state.

Use Back Home to return and confirm the intended cards and entry rows appear.

Owner · Edit On

12. Exchange rates

Open: enable Edit On and select Exchange Rates. This page manages the current USD/INR rate pair and its history.

Set a manual rate

  1. Enter a positive USD to INR rate: the amount of INR for 1 USD.
  2. Select Manual override.
  3. Confirm the current USD to INR, automatically calculated inverse INR to USD, and Last updated value.
Example: entering 83 means 1 USD = 83 INR. The inverse is approximately 0.012048 USD per INR. This is an example, not a current exchange-rate quote.

Refresh a live rate

Select Refresh live rate to request a rate from the configured provider. If the live service is unavailable, the page shows an error; enter an agreed manual rate instead.

Rate history lists recorded currency pairs, values, and whether their source was manual or live. The tracker currently displays revenue in its original USD/INR units; saving an exchange rate does not convert the dashboard or calendar amounts.

Select Back Home to return.
